# gip-garage-pet-bot
 Visitors to Garage@EEE may be interested in the makerspace but may not know what Garage offers, what the different portfolios do, or whom they should approach for help. The Garage Pet Bot aims to make this information more accessible through a small, friendly interactive robot. Visitors will be able to ask questions about Garage and receive approved explanations through voice and visual interaction. The project brings together mechanical design, electronics and software, with scope to explore recognition of selected committee members and later safe low-speed tabletop movement. A stationary and a moving robot will be built under this project.

What You’ll Work Towards
• Design and build a pet-style enclosure integrating the camera, microphone, speaker, display, computing hardware and power system.
• Develop an approved set of responses covering Garage facilities, portfolios and common visitor questions.
• Demonstrate a working voice interaction system that can understand questions and provide relevant responses.
• Develop recognition for selected committee members while handling unknown users appropriately.
• Integrate, test, and document the complete prototype for future operation and improvement.
• Ensure the robot can move around the table without falling off
• Ensure the robot can rotate its body to move around to interact and follow garage members around
