# gip-garage-pet-bot
 Visitors to Garage@EEE may be interested in the makerspace but may not know what Garage offers, what the different portfolios do, or whom they should approach for help. The Garage Pet Bot aims to make this information more accessible through a small, friendly interactive robot. Visitors will be able to ask questions about Garage and receive approved explanations through voice and visual interaction. The project brings together mechanical design, electronics and software, with scope to explore recognition of selected committee members and later safe low-speed tabletop movement. A stationary and a moving robot will be built under this project.
